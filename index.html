WHAT THIS IS

A small, private group pools capital to fund short-term loans. Returns come from voluntary tips, not fixed interest. This is a member-owned collective — not a public offering, and not a bank.

WHAT THIS ISN'T

This tool does not disclose scoring logic, funding criteria, or internal operating methods. Those stay with the team's admins. This walkthrough covers structure and terms only.

STEP 1 OF 6

How a team is built

Every Hustler's Cabin team is capped at 25 seats. Right now, only one team is active — once it's full, a new team forms.

ADMIN

Admin (3 seats)

Oversees the pool, holds banking authority, and approves fund movement. Every transfer requires two of the three admins to sign off — no single admin can move money alone.

ADMIN

Loan Manager

One of the three admins also carries this role. They source and evaluate loan opportunities and bring recommendations to the group — but still can't move funds without a second admin's approval.

MEMBER

Member (22 seats)

Owns shares of the pool, votes on major decisions, and has visibility into performance. Members don't manage funds directly but share in how the pool grows — and can buy additional shares at the current price at any time.

WHY DUAL APPROVAL

Separating "who finds the loan" from "who releases the money" keeps any one person from controlling the pool's funds alone. It's the core safeguard behind every transfer.

WHY A CAP

Scaling loan volume faster than the pool's capital and vetting process can support raises risk for everyone. Twenty loans is the ceiling for this phase — after that, admins reassess before growing further.

Your investment

Joining a team requires a single, one-time contribution at the current share price. There is no recurring fee, though you're welcome to buy additional shares later at whatever the price is then.

WHERE IT GOES

Your contribution joins the shared operating pool, which funds member-approved loans. Profits stay in the pool to grow lending capacity until the group votes to begin distributions.

RETURNS ARE NOT GUARANTEED

This is a private investment among members, not a savings product. Borrowers can default. Value depends on the pool's performance, not a fixed rate. Share price moves with the pool — it can rise or fall.

Terms & non-compete

Read the terms below in full. You'll sign digitally on the next screen.

1. NATURE OF PARTICIPATION

Hustler's Cabin is a private, member-funded lending pool. Membership is by invitation only and is not offered to the general public. Participation is not a deposit account, is not FDIC insured, and does not guarantee any return of principal or profit.

2. GOVERNANCE

Each team consists of up to twenty-five (25) members, including three (3) Admins, one of whom also serves as Loan Manager. No single Admin may authorize the movement of pooled funds; all transfers require approval from at least two (2) Admins.

3. CONTRIBUTION

Membership requires a one-time contribution equal to the current share price at the time of signing, as displayed in Step 4. This is the required investment to join; no further contribution is required to maintain membership, though members may purchase additional shares later at the then-current price.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY

Members will not have access to, and this system will not disclose, internal loan-scoring methodology, underwriting criteria, or operational processes used by the team's Admins. This information is treated as confidential and proprietary to the team.

5. NON-COMPETE & NON-SOLICITATION

By signing below, the undersigned agrees that, during their membership and for a period of twelve (12) months following their exit from the pool, they will not: (a) form, join, or advise a competing member-funded lending pool that solicits current Hustler's Cabin members or admins; (b) solicit current members, admins, or borrowers of Hustler's Cabin to join a competing venture; or (c) use any confidential methodology, scoring logic, documentation, or operational process learned through participation in Hustler's Cabin to build or support a competing venture.

6. RISK ACKNOWLEDGMENT

The undersigned acknowledges that pooled funds are used to fund loans to third parties, that borrowers may default, and that the value of their membership interest — and the share price — may rise, fall, or be lost entirely. This is not a guaranteed-return product.
